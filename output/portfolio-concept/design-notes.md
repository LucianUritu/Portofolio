Image-only proposal. The existing website source was not changed.

## Design plan

Audience: engineering teams and recruiters reviewing Lucian's software engineering work.
Job: understand his focus, inspect projects, and contact him or view his CV.

Palette: midnight #07131C, slate #172B3A, ice #EDF5F7, cobalt #3978FF, copper #D9A17A.
Type direction: Sora display and Manrope body; generated lettering approximates these rather than embedding font files.

Composition, left aligned inside equal margins:

    Name                     Work  About  Contact  CV
    Large identity        Sculptural urban simulation
    Role and introduction        simulation continues
    Explore my work
    Selected work
    MUP publishing preview       Traffic simulation preview
    Gemini-Lite network project
    About                     Skills
    Contact invitation        Social links

Principle: the MPI traffic project's graph partitions become the hero's sculptural computational layers. The proposed live interaction lets a visitor inspect traffic flow with the pointer; touch uses an explicit play/pause control. Content stays visible without the interaction.

Review before generation: replaced an anonymous AI orb with a recognizable urban simulation tied to the portfolio's real HPC project. Rejected the initial contact-sheet, quiet-instrument, and type-only options because they undersold the requested cinematic impact. Used the dark-room composition as inspiration (seed 1877945877), with the custom palette, type, and asymmetric project layout above. No stock photography used.

## Intended motion (not implemented)

- Entry: computational road layers assemble once over about 900 ms. Text remains readable throughout. No loading gate.
- Hero: traffic particles follow road routes; a small play/pause control stops the loop. Pointer movement changes depth slightly, without moving text.
- Scroll: the city tilts subtly into the selected-work composition over a short scroll interval. Native scrolling remains available.
- Project selection: preview expands into its case study with a shared-element transition, about 450 ms. This requires case-study content during any future implementation.
- Links and buttons: restrained 150 ms hover/press feedback; visible keyboard focus.
- Reduced motion: fully formed still city, no looping traffic, no depth travel, instant case-study transition.

Current motion references: [GSAP showcase](https://gsap.com/showcase/) and [DiMaac project demos](https://dimaac.com/projects), reviewed November 10, 2026. These inform the motion direction, not the visual assets.
